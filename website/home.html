01

Talk naturally

Address Jarvis by name and follow up by voice. Speech recognition and speech synthesis can run entirely on your computer.

02

Remember what matters

A local diary and knowledge graph keep context between sessions. Browse saved conversations, preferences and plans in the Memory Viewer.

03

Type when you need to

Companion chat shares your voice conversation and memory. Text replies stay silent, so you can use it without disturbing the room.

04

Dictate into other apps

Hold a shortcut, speak, then release to paste locally transcribed text. History, a custom dictionary and optional cleanup are available where dictation is supported.

05

Get things done

Built-in tools cover web search, weather, time, local files, screenshot OCR and nutrition tracking. Network features connect when you use them.

06

Connect your own tools

MCP servers can add browser automation, smart-home controls and other tools. You choose the servers and what they can access.
